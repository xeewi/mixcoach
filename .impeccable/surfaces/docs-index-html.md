# Surface brief: MixCoach download site (docs/index.html)

Scope: the download page on GitHub Pages. Visitor mode: Persuade. Related surfaces sharing the world: the Ableton panel (m4l/ui.html, Operate) and the HTML report (CSS in mixcoach.py, Operate).

Audience: beginner home-studio producers on Ableton Live 12. Action: download MixCoach.zip and install it. Proof: real findings from the product ("Pad brings 60% of the mud between 250 and 500 Hz"), real screenshots once redone, 18 languages, free/offline. No invented numbers or testimonials.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The mix read as a coded sleeve: six frequency zones, six flat colours, every finding a catalogue number. Refuses the dark plugin-vendor page with a glowing screenshot and a grid of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Flat offset fields on a silver-grey ground, black ink, no gradients, no glow, no rounded cards. The colour cipher is data and only data: sub ultramarine, bass green, low mids yellow, mids vermilion, high mids magenta, air sky blue. Severity is never a colour; it is a black catalogue label (FIX / CHECK / INFO / OK). Neo-grotesk set small and tracked for labels, large and tight for findings. Hairline rules, square corners.

STORY: The visitor sees their own beginner problem named in one sentence, understands that MixCoach names the culprit track per frequency zone, believes it because the sleeve shows the real code, and downloads.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Silver ground. Top-left catalogue mark "MXC 001" plus nav. Left column: a real finding as the headline, a one-line explainer, a black solid download block with the release tag. Right: a square sleeve, six horizontal bands sized by the demo mix's energy, the low-mids band outlined in black and labelled with the culprit track. Hovering/tapping a band swaps the headline for that zone's real finding.

FORM: Factory Records / Peter Saville sleeve code, position 1 of 7 (IMPECCABLE'S PICK, chosen by the user over the roll). seed:70b31b0d7a4a9a78

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Cross-surface reach

Panel and report keep the cipher (zone chips, tonal-balance bars coloured by zone) and the catalogue labels for severity, on a quieter ground: dark graphite in the panel (it sits inside Ableton), silver in the report. System font stacks there (offline, 18 scripts).
